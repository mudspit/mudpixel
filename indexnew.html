About Us

WE LIVE
IN THE DARK
AND MAKE
THINGS GLOW.

Mudpixel is a digital design studio born in the Philippines, built for brands that don't play it safe. We're a collective of illustrators, 3D artists, animators, and strategists who thrive at the intersection of craft and chaos.

We work with the bold, the weird, and the obsessed — extreme sports brands, vice-adjacent culture, ecommerce disruptors, and artists who need a visual identity that actually hits.

No templates. No stock art. Every pixel is made by hand.
